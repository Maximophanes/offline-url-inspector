      Understanding Your Results
      

      The URL Inspector examines a link's hostname, characters, and structure without visiting its destination. It can identify deceptive spellings, unusual characters, suspicious URL syntax, and similarities to expected or saved reference hostnames.
      

      A matching hostname does not guarantee a safe website. The inspector cannot verify who operates a website, assess its reputation, examine its contents, or follow redirects.
      

      Internationalized domain names and visually similar characters are not inherently suspicious. Findings indicate possible similarities or unusual structures, not proof of malicious intent.
      

      Technical Limitations
      The inspector identifies subdomains from hostname structure. It does not use a Public Suffix List to determine which part of a name can be registered, or who owns it. Complete right-to-left visual comparisons are unsupported, and internationalized-domain processing may differ between browsers. The inspector is not a comprehensive phishing detector.
      A character's mapping rule does not always describe the result of processing a complete hostname: characters can combine or change order. Original and readable evidence remain in Technical Details. No hostname comparison can rule out a compromised website.
      

      Hostname terms
      Normalization
      Converting a hostname to the form used for comparison or by the browser. The original spelling is preserved separately.
      IDNA
      Rules for processing internationalized domain names, including character mappings and encoded hostnames.
      UTS #39 and confusable mappings
      Unicode's visual-comparison standard and its character mappings. These are comparison data, not destination hostnames or proof of intent.
      DNS labels and registrable domains
      Labels are the sections between dots. A registrable domain is a name that can be registered under a public suffix; the inspector does not determine that registration or ownership.

      Privacy & Storage
      

      Inspection runs locally without requesting the inspected destination. The app does not save inspected URLs, expected-host text, credentials or tokens. It does not read the clipboard automatically. Your browser or operating system may retain form/session data independently of the app.
      

      Only your Reference Domains list is saved by the app. Browser cleanup, private sessions or storage restrictions can remove or prevent it. Exported JSON backups let you restore the list elsewhere.
