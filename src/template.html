        Understanding Your Results

        The inspector examines a URL’s hostname, characters, and structure without visiting the destination. It can identify misleading spellings, suspicious URL syntax, and differences from expected or reference hostnames.

        A matching hostname does not guarantee a safe website. The inspector cannot verify ownership, reputation, compromise, page content, or redirects. Reference domains are comparison targets, not verified trustworthy websites.

        Internationalized names can be legitimate. Unicode confusable mappings and font-dependent ASCII shapes do not establish malicious intent. A subdomain relationship is structural; no registrable-domain or public-suffix ownership inference is made. Full right-to-left visual comparison is unavailable. Browser-dependent IDNA processing can differ. This is not comprehensive phishing detection.

        Privacy & Storage

        Inspection runs locally. The application does not intentionally persist inspected URLs, expected-host text, credentials, or tokens. It never visits submitted destinations or reads the clipboard automatically.

        Only your Reference Domains list is saved in browser storage. Browser cleanup, private sessions, or storage restrictions can remove or prevent it. Exported JSON backups are the portable recovery mechanism.
