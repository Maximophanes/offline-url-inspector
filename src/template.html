            Understanding Your Results
            

            Checks run offline without opening the destination. Eligible hostnames are compared with a bundled list of popular domains. Expected Hostname and your saved references add comparisons you choose.
            

            Resemblance is not proof of malicious intent. No match, or a match with a popular domain, does not verify safety.
            

            Technical Limitations
            Embedded address formats & limits
            Checks look for HTTP and HTTPS addresses in paths, query values and fragments, with up to two layers of URL decoding and bounded nested analysis. Relative addresses are recorded without assuming a base URL; addresses without a scheme remain unresolved.
            Base64, Base64URL, JSON, JWT, encrypted and proprietary formats are not decoded. Server responses and webpage scripts are not examined. Finding no supported address does not rule out hidden destinations. Repeated entries and alternative interpretations do not establish unique final destinations.
            The built-in Majestic popular-domain list is not comprehensive. Unicode, ASCII, spelling and structural comparisons have different eligibility limits. Results distinguish completed checks, unsupported comparisons, interrupted checks and unavailable data.
            A pinned offline Public Suffix List (PSL), with ICANN and PRIVATE rules, identifies suffix and registrable-domain boundaries for automatic comparisons, including private hosting boundaries. These rules do not establish ownership, registration status, availability or legitimacy. Expected Hostname and personal references retain their separate full-hostname comparison rules.
            The inspector cannot establish who operates a website, check reputation, inspect remote contents or follow redirects. Addresses inside a URL are text evidence; no redirect is verified.
            Full right-to-left (RTL) and bidirectional (bidi) visual comparisons remain unsupported. Exact comparisons remain available. Internationalized-domain processing can differ between browsers. Character mappings can combine or reorder when applied to a complete hostname; technical evidence preserves the original spelling and supported comparison methods.
            

            Hostname terms
            Normalization
            Converting a hostname to the form used for comparison or by the browser. The original spelling is preserved separately.
            IDNA
            Rules for processing internationalized domain names, including character mappings and encoded hostnames.
            UTS #39 and confusable mappings
            Unicode's visual-comparison standard and its character mappings. These are comparison data, not destination hostnames or proof of intent.
            Public Suffix List and registrable domains
            The offline PSL lists suffix rules, including ICANN and PRIVATE hosting rules. It identifies website-name boundaries, not whether a domain is registered or who owns it. Labels are the sections between dots.

            Privacy & Storage
            

            Inspection runs locally without requesting the inspected destination. The app does not save inspected URLs, expected-host text, credentials or tokens. It does not read the clipboard automatically. Your browser or operating system may retain form/session data independently of the app.
            

            Only your Reference Domains list is saved by the app. Browser cleanup, private sessions or storage restrictions can remove or prevent it. Exported JSON backups let you restore the list elsewhere.
